# Kenya hospital oxygen infrastructure market

## Objective

Assess the deployment potential for OxyTrack across Kenyan hospitals with centralized medical oxygen systems.

## Target hospital categories

### Level 4 hospitals

County hospitals

### Level 5 hospitals

County referral hospitals

### Level 6 hospitals

National referral and teaching hospitals

## Primary customers

* biomedical engineering departments
* hospital administrators
* county health departments
* oxygen plant operators
* hospital maintenance units

## Current challenges

Hospitals often lack:

* ward-level oxygen visibility
* centralized telemetry
* consumption analytics
* leak detection systems
* predictive maintenance tools
* infrastructure performance dashboards

## OxyTrack deployment model

### Phase 1

Level 6 hospitals

### Phase 2

Level 5 hospitals

### Phase 3

Level 4 hospitals

## Value proposition

### Biomedical engineers

Real-time infrastructure monitoring

### Hospital management

Cost reduction and operational visibility

### County governments

Asset management and oxygen planning

## Competitive positioning

Traditional medical gas providers focus on infrastructure installation.

OxyTrack focuses on infrastructure intelligence through sensors, telemetry, analytics, and predictive monitoring.

## Revenue model

* hardware deployment
* installation
* annual maintenance
* cloud dashboard subscription
* analytics subscription
* enterprise reporting

## Strategic vision

OxyTrack aims to become the digital monitoring layer for hospital oxygen infrastructure across Kenya and Africa.
