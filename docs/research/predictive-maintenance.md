# Predictive maintenance for hospital oxygen infrastructure

## Objective

Use continuous telemetry data to predict oxygen infrastructure failures before they cause operational disruptions.

## Maintenance challenge

Hospital oxygen systems are commonly maintained through:

* scheduled maintenance
* reactive maintenance
* emergency repair

These approaches may not detect gradual infrastructure degradation.

## OxyTrack strategy

Predictive maintenance uses:

* pressure trends
* flow trends
* sensor diagnostics
* regulator behavior
* valve performance
* communication health

## Predictive indicators

### Pressure instability

Frequent pressure fluctuations

### Regulator degradation

Progressive pressure loss

### Valve restriction

Reduced downstream flow

### Pipeline deterioration

Abnormal pressure differential

### Sensor reliability

Increased calibration drift

## Health scoring

Each infrastructure component receives a dynamic health score.

Health Score = Pressure Stability + Flow Stability + Sensor Reliability + Historical Performance
