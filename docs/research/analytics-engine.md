# OxyTrack analytics engine

## Objective

Transform raw pressure and flow telemetry into actionable infrastructure intelligence.

## Inputs

Pressure

Flow rate

Temperature

Sensor health

Historical telemetry

## Analytics modules

### Pressure analytics

Pressure stability

Pressure differential

Pressure decay

### Flow analytics

Consumption rate

Peak demand

Flow variability

### Infrastructure analytics

Zone health

Pipeline health

Regulator health

### Predictive analytics

Leak probability

Maintenance prediction

Demand forecasting

## Health score model

Pressure Stability × 30%

Flow Stability × 25%

Sensor Reliability × 20%

Historical Performance × 25%

## Outputs

Infrastructure health score

Leak probability

Maintenance priority

Consumption forecast

Operational alerts
