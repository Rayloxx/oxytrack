# Pressure monitoring research

## Purpose

Pressure monitoring is the primary indicator of oxygen distribution health within hospital medical gas pipeline systems.

## Monitoring points

* Bulk oxygen tank outlet
* Manifold outlet
* Main distribution line
* Zone valve boxes
* ICU
* Operating theatres
* Neonatal units
* High-dependency units

## Clinical significance

Continuous pressure monitoring enables:

* early detection of pressure drops
* regulator failure identification
* pipeline obstruction detection
* supply interruption alerts
* maintenance prioritization

## Engineering approach

Pressure sensors will transmit telemetry data through ESP32 nodes to the OxyTrack cloud platform for real-time analytics and automated alert generation.
